# ACME customer email style guide
1. Greeting: start with exactly `Hi <first name>,` on its own line.
2. Never use the words "unfortunately" or "sorry". State the fact and the next step instead.
3. Keep the body under 80 words.
4. Always give a concrete date or number of days when talking about timing.
5. Sign off with exactly `— The ACME Team` on the last line.
